Our story

Star Cinema opened its doors in 2010 with a simple idea: a friendly place to enjoy great films. Today we show new releases every day across four screens.

Dolby Atmos sound in Screens 3 and 4, standard seats and a comfortable recliner row at the back and a licensed café bar make every visit special.
