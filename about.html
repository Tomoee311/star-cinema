Our story

Star Cinema opened its doors in 2010 with a simple idea: a friendly place to enjoy great films. Today we show new releases every day across four screens.

Dolby Atmos sound in Screens 3 and 4, comfortable recliner rows and a licensed café bar make every visit special.

Facilities

Four screens

Dolby Atmos in Screens 3 and 4. Screens 1 and 2 show in standard sound.

Licensed café bar

Snacks, coffee and drinks before the film.

Step-free access

Lifts and ramps on every level.
